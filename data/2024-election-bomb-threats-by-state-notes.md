# 2024 November Election Bomb Threats by State

## Scope

This dataset summarizes the Brennan Center for Justice's `2024 Election Bomb
Threat Tracker`, last updated March 28, 2025. The tracker covers publicly
confirmed bomb threats targeting polling locations, election offices, and
tabulation centers on Election Day and in the days after.

The state totals are publicly confirmed minimums, not verified exhaustive
counts. The tracker itself notes that its public-information research may not
be exhaustive.

## Important Counting Notes

- The tracker counts documented threats or targeted election facilities, not
  necessarily unique polling locations. For example, Georgia's Fulton County
  row records 32 threats while contemporary reporting described five affected
  polling places there.
- The tracker records four Michigan county polling-location threats. CNN
  reporting citing Michigan Secretary of State Jocelyn Benson described five
  Michigan locations when the Secretary of State office in Lansing is
  included. The CSV preserves the Brennan tracker minimum so its total
  reconciles to 227 and records the supplemental Michigan report in the note.
- The FBI stated on November 5, 2024 that many polling-location threats
  appeared to originate from Russian email domains and that none had been
  determined credible at that time. This is narrower than claiming that every
  threat in the tracker was attributable to Russia.
