Fall 2012 General Election Results

Date: 
November 6, 2012

Official results of the November 6, 2012 General Election for President, U.S. Senator, Representative to Congress, State Senator, State Representative, and District Attorney, certified November 29, 2012.

In addition to the regular reports, a spreadsheet of ward-by-ward presidential results with the corresponding State Senate and State Assembly districts, is attached at the bottom of this page.

Note: The term "scattering"refers to write-in votes for unregistered candidates.
