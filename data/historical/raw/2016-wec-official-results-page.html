2016 Fall General Election Results

Date: 
November 8, 2016

Official results of the November 8, 2016 General Election for President of the United States (recount), U.S. Senator, Representative to Congress, State Senator (SD32 recount), State Representative and District Attorney, certified November 29, 2016.

NOTE: President of the President of the United States (http://elections.wi.gov/node/4460) and for State Senate District 32 were subject to recounts.

Note: The term "scattering"refers to write-in votes for unregistered candidates.
