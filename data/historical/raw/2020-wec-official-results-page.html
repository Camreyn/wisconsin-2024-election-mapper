2020 Fall General Election Results

Date

Tue, 12/01/2020 - 13:00

Official results of the November 3, 2020, General Election for President of the United States (partial recount), Representative to Congress, State Senator, State Representative and District Attorney.  Presidential results were canvassed on November 30 and certified by the Governor the same day. Presidential results are still subject to change due to litigation.  All other races were certified on December 1, 2020.

NOTE: The election for President of the United States was subject to a partial recount in Dane and Milwaukee counties.
